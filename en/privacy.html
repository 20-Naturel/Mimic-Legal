Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

In short: Mimic reads messages in the channels it monitors to spot ones that might be a problem. It keeps a 90-day history of alerts, with an excerpt of the flagged message, visible only to moderators. It sells nothing and shares nothing with third parties (except the technical providers listed below). Reports stay on your Discord server.

This English version is provided for convenience. The French version prevails.

1. Who is responsible

Mimic is developed and run by the Natural 20 team, which is the data controller for the processing described here. For any question or request, contact argannao on the Discord server where Mimic is installed.

2. What Mimic reads

On servers where it is installed and monitoring is enabled, Mimic receives messages posted in the channels it can see, except those the administrators chose to ignore. It analyzes their text when they are posted or edited, to spot:

  • watched words or phrases (insults, hate speech, threats, harassment);
  • suspicious links (shortened links, fake sites, scams);
  • spam (repeated messages, flooding, mass mentions);
  • sensitive topics (sexual violence, child sexual abuse, incest, bestiality).

To judge whether an insult is a joke, Mimic also looks at the context: reactions to the message, the reply from the person targeted, and the mood of the channel in the previous few minutes.

4. Reports

When a message is flagged, Mimic posts a report in a channel chosen by the server's administrators. The report contains the author's username, ID and avatar, the channel, an excerpt of the message (up to 1,000 characters), the reason and the context clues.

This report is a Discord message posted on the same server. It stays under the control of that server's administrators, who can delete it at any time. Mimic keeps a record of it in the alert history (see the table above), available on the website for 90 days to people who can access the server's settings.

5. The settings website

The website mimic.argannao.com lets you configure Mimic. You sign in with Discord: Mimic only asks for your identity (ID, username and avatar), never your messages, your server list or your email address.

This identity is kept in a signed session cookie (mimic_session) on your device, until you sign out and for 7 days at most. This cookie is required for the site to work. There are no analytics or advertising cookies.

A server's settings can be accessed by members with the "Manage Server" permission, by members they authorized on the site, and by the Natural 20 team for technical support. The site shows this server's alert history, excerpts included; for sensitive topics, the excerpt is hidden by default. It shows no other messages. When settings are changed, the person's Discord ID is written to the machine's technical logs, which are deleted automatically over time.

6. Why: legal basis

This processing is based on legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): helping moderators keep the server safe and pleasant for everyone. Mimic makes no automated decisions: it deletes no messages and punishes no one.

7. Sharing

Mimic sells no data and shares none with third parties, except the technical providers below. It sends no messages to any external artificial intelligence service. Data passes through Discord, which applies its own privacy policy.

Mimic is hosted in France, on a machine managed by the Natural 20 team. The settings website goes through Cloudflare, which manages the domain name and the secure connection: Cloudflare sees visits to the site, but not Discord messages.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, and object to its processing. To do so, contact argannao on the Discord server where Mimic is installed. If you no longer have access to that server, open a ticket on GitHub: it is public, so only give your Discord username and we will get back to you. We reply within one month.

You can also ask your server's administrators to delete a report about you, or to exclude a channel from monitoring.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL, or your local authority.

9. Minimum age

Mimic is meant for Discord users and follows the minimum ages set by Discord's Terms of Service.

10. Changes

This policy may change, for example when Mimic gains new features. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced to the administrators of servers using Mimic.
