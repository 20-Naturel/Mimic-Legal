Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

In short: Mimic reads messages in the channels it monitors to spot ones that might be a problem. It stores no message content on disk, sells nothing and shares nothing with third parties. Reports stay on your Discord server.

This English version is provided for convenience. The French version prevails.

1. Who is responsible

Mimic is developed and run by the Natural 20 team, which is the data controller for the processing described here. For any question or request, contact argannao on Discord.

2. What Mimic reads

On servers where it is installed and monitoring is enabled, Mimic receives messages posted in the channels it can see, except those the administrators chose to ignore. It analyzes their text when they are posted or edited, to spot:

  • watched words or phrases (insults, hate speech, threats, harassment);
  • suspicious links (shortened links, fake sites, scams);
  • spam (repeated messages, flooding, mass mentions).

To judge whether an insult is a joke, Mimic also looks at the context: reactions to the message, the reply from the person targeted, and the mood of the channel in the previous few minutes.

4. Reports

When a message is flagged, Mimic posts a report in a channel chosen by the server's administrators. The report contains the author's username, ID and avatar, the channel, an excerpt of the message (up to 1,000 characters), the reason and the context clues.

This report is a Discord message posted on the same server. It stays under the control of that server's administrators, who can delete it at any time. Mimic keeps no copy.

5. Why: legal basis

This processing is based on legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): helping moderators keep the server safe and pleasant for everyone. Mimic makes no automated decisions: it deletes no messages and punishes no one.

6. Sharing

Mimic sells no data and shares none with third parties. It sends no messages to any external artificial intelligence service. Data passes through Discord, which applies its own privacy policy.

Mimic is hosted in France, on a machine managed by the Natural 20 team.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, and object to its processing. To do so, contact argannao on Discord and name the server concerned. We reply within one month.

You can also ask your server's administrators to delete a report about you, or to exclude a channel from monitoring.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL, or your local authority.

8. Minimum age

Mimic is meant for Discord users and follows the minimum ages set by Discord's Terms of Service.

9. Changes

This policy may change, for example when Mimic gains new features. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced to the administrators of servers using Mimic.
