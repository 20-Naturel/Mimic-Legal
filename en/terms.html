1. The service

Mimic is a free Discord bot developed by the Natural 20 team. It helps moderators of tabletop RPG servers by reporting messages that might be a problem. By adding Mimic to a server or using its commands, you accept these terms.

2. What Mimic does, and doesn't do

Mimic reports: it posts alerts in a channel chosen by the administrators. It deletes no messages, and kicks or punishes no one. All moderation decisions are made by humans.

Mimic can be wrong: it may flag a harmless message, or miss a problematic one. Its reports and alert levels are hints, not evidence or judgments.

3. Administrators' responsibilities

Administrators who install Mimic on a server agree to:

  • tell the server's members that their messages are analyzed by a moderation bot;
  • keep the report channel for the people in charge of moderation;
  • review reports carefully before taking any action;
  • follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

4. Prohibited uses

You may not use Mimic to monitor or harass a specific person, try to disrupt it, bypass its protections, or access data that doesn't concern you.

5. Availability

Mimic is a community project provided as is, with no guarantee of availability or results. It may be interrupted, changed or stopped at any time, for example for maintenance. The Natural 20 team may remove Mimic from a server that doesn't follow these terms.

6. Liability

To the extent permitted by law, the Natural 20 team is not responsible for decisions moderators make based on Mimic's reports, or for the consequences of a message Mimic did not catch.

7. Personal data

How Mimic uses data is described in the Privacy Policy.
